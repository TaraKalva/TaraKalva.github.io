Store Performance Analysis — Global Superstore

A comprehensive Tableau profitability and discount-sensitivity analysis across 9,994 retail transactions and 21 operational dimensions to isolate margin leakage and guide pricing strategy.

Business Objective

High top-line sales often mask unprofitable discounting and regional fulfillment drag. This project examines 9,994 Global Superstore orders across 21 features to separate revenue volume from true net profitability.

Category Action Plan

Delivered targeted pricing and product-mix recommendations to restore sustainable profitability.

Strategic Recommendations

  • Cap Unprofitable Discount Tiers: Align promotional thresholds with category margin cushions so volume gains do not produce negative net profit.
  • Rebalance Regional Product Mix: Prioritize high-margin sub-categories in lagging territories while auditing fulfillment and pricing structure for loss-making SKUs.
