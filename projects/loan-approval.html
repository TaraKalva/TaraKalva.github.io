Problem Context & Explainability

In lending and credit underwriting, predictive accuracy alone is not enough—stakeholders and compliance reviewers must understand why an application carries higher risk. By building a logistic regression classifier in R, this project prioritizes transparent probability calibration alongside strong discrimination.

Analytical Process

Phase 01

Exploratory Analysis

Analyzed applicant attributes and class distributions to surface variables correlated with approval outcomes.

Phase 02

Logistic Modeling

Fitted a binomial logistic regression classifier in R to estimate calibrated approval probabilities.

Phase 03

Validation

Achieved 82% validation accuracy and 0.88 ROC-AUC across held-out evaluation samples.

Phase 04

Odds Ratio Insights

Exponentiated model coefficients into odds ratios to communicate concrete risk multipliers.

Outcomes & Decision Impact

  • Strong Discrimination: 0.88 ROC-AUC demonstrates clean separation between approved and high-risk profiles across probability thresholds.
  • Actionable Risk Drivers: Translating log-odds coefficients into plain-language odds ratios enables analysts to explain exactly how individual applicant factors shift approval likelihood.
