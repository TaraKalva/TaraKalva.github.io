TicketLens — Automated AI Ticket Triage & Pattern Detection

An end-to-end SaaS support triage system that classifies and routes incoming tickets with LLMs and rule-based fallbacks, escalates low-confidence predictions to a human review queue, flags surging incident categories, and forecasts the next probable support ticket.

Executive Summary & Problem Statement

High-volume support teams face two compounding bottlenecks: manual ticket routing delays time-to-resolution, while purely automated classifiers silently misroute ambiguous incidents. At the same time, reactive dashboards rarely warn operations leads before a specific category spikes into a broader outage.

TicketLens solves both challenges in a unified operational workflow. Every incoming ticket is scored with structured classification, confidence calibration, and one-line reasoning. High-confidence tickets route immediately, uncertain tickets pause in a human-in-the-loop Review Queue, and background analytics continuously scan rolling 7-day windows to surface emerging operational risk.

System Architecture & Triage Pipeline

Historical records and live submissions share a unified SQLite schema seeded automatically from 100,000 synthetic IT support tickets (`better-sqlite3`), enabling instant analytics alongside real-time AI inference.

Step 01

Ticket Ingestion

Users submit ticket descriptions via the React UI; records enter the Express API pipeline.

Step 02

AI Triage Engine

Gemini or OpenAI classifies category, severity, team, confidence score, and reasoning—with rule-based fallback if no key is set.

Step 03

Confidence Gate

Predictions below 0.7 trigger needsReview, routing to the human Review Queue for confirmation or correction.

Step 04

Cron Risk & Forecast

Every 15 minutes, node-cron flags ≥25% 7-day volume surges and generates a grounded next-ticket forecast.

Key Capabilities & Technical Design

  • Resilient Dual-Mode AI Inference: Auto-detects Google Gemini or OpenAI API keys and seamlessly falls back to a deterministic keyword-rule classifier so end-to-end triage works reliably even without external API keys.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Governance: Rather than blindly trusting low-certainty outputs, tickets with confidence under CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD (0.7) enter the Review Queue where analysts confirm or override assignments.
  • Proactive Trend Risk Detection: Compares each category's last 7 days against the prior 7 days (with a minimum volume floor to suppress low-traffic noise), flagging rising categories with plain-language recommendations.
  • Grounded Next-Ticket Forecasting: Feeds live volume trends and real recent ticket samples from the highest-signal category into the model to forecast probable incoming issues with rationale.
  • 100k-Scale Paginated Explorer: Supports server-side pagination, full-text search, and filtering across status, category, team, and review state via GET /api/tickets.

Unified Ticket Taxonomy

Historical CSV imports and newly triaged submissions normalize into eight categories, four severity tiers, and eight routing teams:

Severity Levels (4) Low, Medium, High, Critical
Routing Teams (8) Mobile App, Identity & Access, Platform/API, Billing, Data, Analytics, Notifications, Product

Technology Stack & Deployment

  • Frontend: React (Vite), Tailwind CSS, Chart.js (Dashboard, SubmitTicket, ReviewQueue, AllTickets).
  • Backend & Scheduling: Node.js, Express, SQLite via better-sqlite3, node-cron background telemetry.
  • Hosting: Configured via Render Blueprint (render.yaml) with automatic database seeding on startup.
